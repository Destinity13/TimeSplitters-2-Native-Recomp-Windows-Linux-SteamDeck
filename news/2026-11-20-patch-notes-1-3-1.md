# Crystal Cut 1.3.1 brings automatic updates back

Everyone on 1.2.x or 1.3.0 can now update straight from the launcher. Just start it and press Update. Your saves and settings are kept, and the game is linked once more automatically (takes a few minutes).

No new content in this one. It's the same Crystal Cut you know from 1.3.0, now reachable for everyone who didn't see the new release. Fresh installs can grab the full package from the [release page](https://github.com/Destinity13/TimeSplitters-2-Native-Recomp-Windows-Linux-SteamDeck/releases/tag/v1.3.1) as usual.

Next up was a widescreen patch for the crosshair and the light glows. That one is out now as [1.3.2](2026-11-21-patch-notes-1-3-2.md).
