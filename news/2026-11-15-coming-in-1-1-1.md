# Coming soon: update 1.1.1

15 November 2026

A quick fix update is almost ready. Here is what it brings.

## Weapons and mouse aiming

- Sniper Rifle and Vintage Rifle scopes work properly again.
- The Soviet S47 no longer stays zoomed in.
- Tap the zoom key to open the scope, tap again to close it. Holding still works.
- New switch: auto aim for the mouse, off by default.

## Optional cheats

Infinite health, armor and ammo for single player and split screen. Off by default and never active in Online+.

## Online+

Your own kills count on the scoreboard, hit markers are clearer and the small hitch twice a second is gone.

## Windows setup stuck at 68 %

A few of you reported that the setup on Windows stops at 68 % and never finishes. That is the last step, where the game is linked together. On our own PCs it never happened, so we could not watch it go wrong.

What we expect: on some PCs that step gets stuck while it runs on all cores at once. 1.1.1 leaves the normal way as it is, but if the step takes longer than 15 minutes, the setup stops it and tries again by itself on a single core. On our test laptop that second try built the game without problems.

Because we could not reproduce the error itself, we can't promise this fixes it for everyone. If your setup still hangs, please open an issue on GitHub and attach `app/build/setup.log`. That helps us a lot.

## Steam Deck

Good news for everyone on the Deck. The launcher did not open on SteamOS so far, so you got the old setup dialogs and no updates. With 1.1.1 the launcher works on the Deck too, in Desktop Mode and in Game Mode, with news and updates like on every other PC. The mouse also works right away with Steam Input, without the wake up click.

**Steam Deck users need to download 1.1.1 once by hand.** After that, all future updates come through the launcher.

## How you get it

On 1.1.0 on Windows or Linux, the launcher offers the update as soon as it is out. Your saves are backed up first.
