# Weapons with mouse aiming - we are working on a fix

15 November 2026

Thanks for the first reports on 1.1.0. One topic came up right away, so here is where it stands.

## What is wrong in 1.1.0

With mouse aiming some weapons do not behave the way they should:

- **Sniper Rifle:** after switching to another weapon and back, the scope can come up once by itself.
- **Vintage Rifle:** the scope can get stuck or flicker.
- **Scope in general:** a short tap on the zoom key only flashes the scope up.

The classic controls with a gamepad are not affected.

## What we are doing

We are working on the fix as fast as possible and are going through all weapons, not only the two rifles.

If you are on 1.1.0, you do not have to do anything. The launcher will offer the update as soon as it is ready.

Found a weapon that feels wrong? Open an issue on GitHub, name the level and the weapon and attach `bridge.log`.
