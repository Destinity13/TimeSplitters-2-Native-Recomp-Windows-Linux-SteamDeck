# Version 1.1.1 - patch notes

On a Steam Deck, download 1.1.1 once by hand: the launcher did not open on SteamOS before, so it could not update. From now on it does.

If you are on 1.1.0 anywhere else, the launcher offers this update by itself. Only the changed files are downloaded, your saves are backed up first, and then the game is rebuilt once from your disc. That takes a few minutes.

## Weapons and mouse aiming

- The port now reads the weapon in your hand straight from the game. All 30 weapons are known by name.
- Fixed: Sniper Rifle bringing the scope up by itself after switching weapons.
- Fixed: Vintage Rifle scope getting stuck or flickering.
- Fixed: Soviet S47 and similar weapons staying zoomed in with mouse aiming, also right after picking them up.
- Fixed: with mouse aiming the character got off turrets right away (dam boss in Siberia, Planet X). Turrets can now be steered with the mouse.
- Fixed: the mouse crosshair showed up in cutscenes.
- Fixed: the mouse could stop working after a character healed you above your maximum health (Neo Tokyo).
- Fixed: after using the Sniper Rifle, the S47 and other weapons could zoom in when aiming.
- Fixed: through the sniper scope the picture was brighter and more washed out than on the PS2, and on wide screens the edges showed a shifted copy of an older picture. The scope now looks like the original.
- Fixed: zoomed in through a scope, the mouse turned far too fast. Mouse speed now follows the zoom level.
- New: tap the zoom key to open the scope, tap again to close it. Holding the key still works.
- New: "Auto-aim (mouse)" in the launcher under Controls. Off by default, so shots go exactly to the crosshair. On, the aim is pulled to enemies like on the PS2.

## Picture

- Clearer picture at higher resolutions: the game's haze overlay looked like a second, shifted image (double vision in the distance and while turning). It is now off by default. You can turn it back on in the in-game menu under Picture ("Original haze overlay").
- A copy of the frame the game draws onto itself every frame now lines up exactly at every resolution.

## Cheats (optional, off by default)

- Infinite health, infinite armor and infinite ammo. Switches in the launcher under Controls and in the in-game menu.
- For single player and split screen. They are off in Online+ and in online matches.

## Steam Deck

- The launcher now opens on the Deck, in Desktop Mode and in Game Mode. It brings its own WebKitGTK, which SteamOS does not have. Nothing to install.
- Launch option `TS2_DIREKT=1 %command%` starts the game directly without the launcher.
- Fixed: with Steam Input set to "joystick mouse", the mouse had to be woken up with a click after starting the game and after every pause.

## Launcher

- If the package is incomplete (wrong download or partly extracted), the launcher and the start script now say so instead of stopping without a message.
- Fixed: settings changed in the in-game menu did not show up in the launcher until it was restarted, and saving in the launcher could undo them.

## Linux

- Fixed: with the picture effects "Clear" or "Cinematic" the game could crash right at the start if its folder name has a space in it.

## Online+

- Fixed: your own kills missing on the scoreboard and showing as a suicide in the kill feed.
- Hit and kill markers are bigger and stay on screen a little longer, the hit sound is louder.
- The hit detection no longer causes a small hitch twice a second.

## Setup on Windows

- If the last setup step hangs (it stopped at 68 % for some people), the setup now ends that step after 15 minutes and tries other ways by itself: on a single core with a fresh build cache after stopping leftovers of an earlier, aborted setup, and then in the Windows temp folder. The normal way is unchanged. We could not reproduce the hang ourselves, so we can't promise it fixes it for everyone.
